Theta Decay

You decide to be rational and take this unearned destiny to the bank. You manage your money responsibly, buying the index, owning a house, spending within reason. After a month or two of the dust settling, it finally hits you: you won! You did it!

But then a second thought hits you: what exactly have you done? You were born into this world desiring agency. You have wants and needs and they change from moment to moment. The ability to satiate these needs is what brings you happiness. It wasn't long until you learned you can't do what you want. Confused, you investigate, only to learn it's because of something called money. From that day forward the objective changed, from one of agency to one of monetary wealth. And now, after all these years, you come face to face with your childhood self. And they ask you:

"What now?"

You've spent your life fighting for agency, and in the process, you left yourself no time to ponder how to use it. You have no hobbies. No passions. You were orchestrated by the pursuit of money and yet, now that you have it, you realize you never became a person who knew how to use it.
