Theta Decay

Your time has come. You wonder if there was another option. Did you do everything you could? Was it ever up to you? You know by now of course none of that matters. Eventually, you realize its all noise. No thought about anything can keep you from where you're headed. Money, agency, happiness, they're all just mountains. Sure you can't avoid them, but seeing a vista for its mountains and missing its foilage, is just, like a stupid way to look at a vista. In your final moments it's not imagery of balance sheets and DJIA returns that flash through your head. Instead its a lot of moments you haven't thought of in a long time. The days spent working shoulder to shoulder with a girl you liked, always to afraid to say anything. The nights you spent wondering what would have happened if you kept your band together after highschool. The afternoons you spent wondering if your friends all actually hated you, instead of just asking them.

You're whole life was dictated by money. But you never had a choice in that. Maybe you should have spent less time looking at the big picture, and more time looking at the moment that's been given to you.
