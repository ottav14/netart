Theta Decay

You beg for change until you have enough for a $10 game. Scratching away the foil with your last nickel you hold your breathe in anticipation.

You lost. What did you think this is? Defeated, you collapse onto your carboard floor. It's in this moment you accept it all. Everything God has shown you from day one. You allow it in. And you accept.

Nothing much crosses your mind. There's little left to think. The last use of agency you provide yourself is a weak, self-assured grin. Paramedics find your body on break while going to grab some Hi-Chew.
